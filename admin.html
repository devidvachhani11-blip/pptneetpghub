Top 10 (anonymous)

Students see only a member ID and a score. No names, no emails. Only each student's first attempt counts. Scores are worked out again from the saved answers. Ties go to the student with fewer wrong answers, then the earlier attempt.

Card series

Up to 10 MB each. PNG, JPG or WebP.

Recent drops (home page)

Up to 3 lines shown to visitors on the home page, under "Recent drops". You type them yourself. Nothing is read from your resources. Newest date shows first. Leave a row empty to hide it. If all 3 are empty, the card is hidden.

Real Test (GT style)

Full exam: 180 MCQs in 5 groups (A to E) of 36. 42 minutes per group, 3 h 30 m in all. +4 correct, −1 wrong, 0 skipped. Maximum 720. Students cannot go back to a finished group or open the next one early.

Headings in row 1: Subject (optional), Question, A, B, C, D, Answer, Explanation. Questions 1 to 36 go to Group A, 37 to 72 to Group B, and so on. They fill the 5 boxes below, so you can still change anything. Download the template

Same format as Add Test: Q1. question, options A to D, Answer: B, Explanation: ... You can use both ways together. An Excel file only replaces the boxes when you choose it.

For X-rays and slides: choose the picture files here, then write Image: xray1.png under that question in its box.
