    Quick answers.

    How do I pay?

    Scan the QR code or pay to the UPI ID from any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and others). Then enter your 12-digit UTR in the steps above.

    Where do I find my UTR?

    Open the payment in your UPI app and look for the UTR or UPI transaction ID. It is a 12-digit number.

    When does my access start?

    We check every payment by hand against our UPI account, so it can take a little while at busy times. Once approved, sign in with the same Google account and your library opens.

    How do I get the Telegram channels?

    Telegram Premium is optional and included: the Premium channel, the INICET Bonus channel and the discussion group. Once you are a member, get your invite links from your dashboard.

    I entered the wrong UTR. What now?

    If we can't match it, you will see a message here and can submit again. You can also message us on Telegram or WhatsApp.

    Which email gets access?

    The Google account you sign in with. Use the one you will study with.

    Can I download the PDFs?

    Notes and tests are view-only. Flash cards are downloadable.
